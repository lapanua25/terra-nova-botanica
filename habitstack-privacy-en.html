Habit Stack Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 ・ 日本語はこちら

Habit Stack (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information.

In short: the App collects no information and sends nothing to the developer.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information, including information that could identify you. It does not collect or send your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers (such as IDFA), or analytics about how you use the App. No account is needed.

2. Your habit records

The names, colors, icons and reminder times of your routines and stacks (new habits), and your daily check-offs, are all stored on your device. No one, including the developer, can see or access them. If you delete the App or use "Settings > Reset all data", the data on your device is deleted.

3. iCloud backup

To help when you change phones, the App saves your data and settings once a day to your own iCloud Drive, in an area used only by the App. This area belongs to your Apple Account, and the developer cannot see or access it. Nothing is saved if you're not signed in to iCloud or iCloud Drive is off.

The App's data is also included in your iPhone's iCloud backup.

"Settings > Export to a file" exports your records to a single file. You choose where to save it (the Files app and so on). It is never sent to the developer.

4. Reminders

The App uses local notifications that are handled entirely on your device. It does not contact any server to send reminders. You can change notification permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases of Pro are processed by Apple (App Store). The developer does not receive or store your payment information. Purchases and refunds are handled under Apple's terms.

6. Ads

The App shows no ads, so it does not collect any information for advertising.

7. Sharing with third parties

As described above, the App collects no information, so there is nothing to share with third parties.

8. Links to other services

The App links to a contact form (Google Forms). Information you enter there is handled under that service provider's privacy policy.

9. Children's privacy

Because the App collects no information, it is safe to use at any age.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, use "Settings > Help & contact" in the App, or the support page.
