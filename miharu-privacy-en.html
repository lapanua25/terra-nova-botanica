Miharu Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

日本語

Miharu ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information.

In short: the App collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information, including information that could identify you. It does not collect or send your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers (such as IDFA), or usage analytics. No account is required.

2. What you enter

Everything you enter in the App (item names, amounts, how often you pay, start and end months), your monthly bills, review ideas, and the optional take-home pay amount are stored only on your device. Neither the developer nor any third party can see or access them. Deleting the App removes this data from your device.

3. iCloud backup

To help when you change phones, the App saves your data and settings to your own iCloud Drive, in an area used only by the App (you can turn off automatic backup in Settings). This area belongs to your Apple ID, and the developer cannot see or access it.

The App’s data is also included in your iPhone’s iCloud backup.

4. Banks and cards

The App does not connect to bank accounts, credit cards, or payment services. All amounts are entered by you, and the App never moves real money.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases of Pro features are processed by Apple (App Store). The developer never receives or stores your payment information. Purchases and refunds follow Apple’s policies.

6. Advertising

The App shows no ads, so it does not collect any information for advertising.

7. Sharing with third parties

As described above, the App collects no information, so there is nothing to share with third parties.

8. Links to other services

The App links to a contact form (Google Forms). Information you submit there is handled under that service’s privacy policy.

9. Children’s privacy

Because the App collects no information, it is safe to use at any age.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, use “Settings > Contact” in the App, or visit the support page.
