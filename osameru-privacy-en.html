OSAMERU Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

日本語はこちら

OSAMERU ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

In short: the App collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information. It does not collect or send your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers (such as IDFA), or analytics about how you use the App. No account is required.

2. Your budgets and entries

The categories, budget amounts, spending amounts, dates and notes you enter are stored only on your device. Neither the developer nor any third party can see or access them. Deleting the App deletes the data on your device.

3. Moving to a new iPhone with iCloud

To help you move to a new iPhone, the App saves your budgets and entries to your own iCloud Drive, in an area used only by the App (you can turn automatic backup off in Settings). This storage belongs to your Apple ID, and the developer cannot see or access it.

The App's data is also included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup.

4. Home Screen widget

To show the Home Screen widget (Pro), the App writes category names and remaining amounts to a shared area on your device used only by the App. This information never leaves your device.

5. In-app purchase

Purchases of Pro are processed by Apple (App Store). The developer does not receive or store your payment information. Purchases and refunds are handled under Apple's terms.

6. About amounts

Amounts shown in the App are the numbers you enter. The App does not connect to bank accounts or credit cards and never moves real money.

7. Advertising

The App shows no ads, so it collects no information for advertising.

8. Sharing with third parties

Because the App collects no information, there is nothing to share with third parties.

9. Links to other services

The App links to a contact form (Google Forms). Information you enter there is handled under that service's privacy policy.

10. Children's privacy

Because the App collects no information, it is safe for people of any age.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy, use "Settings > Contact" in the App or the support page.
