SOROSORO Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

日本語はこちら

SOROSORO: Cleaning Schedule ("the app") is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does, and does not do, with information.

In short: the app collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer. It does not use your location, camera, photos or maps.

1. Information we collect

None. The developer does not collect or receive any personal information — no name, email address, or phone number; no location; no device identifiers such as the IDFA; and no analytics, usage, or behavioural data. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no analytics tools are built in. The developer runs no servers for the app.

The app does not use location, the camera, the photo library or maps.

2. Your chores and history

The chore names, places and target intervals you add, and the dates you did each chore, are stored on your iPhone. Neither the developer nor any third party can see them.

All dates you mark as done are kept on your device, with no limit. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

3. Notifications

All notifications are local notifications created on your iPhone. There is no push notification server, and nothing is sent anywhere to deliver them. You can turn notifications off in Settings.

4. Moving to a new iPhone with iCloud

So you can move to a new iPhone, the app backs up your data once a day to your own iCloud, in a space only this app uses (you can turn this off in Settings). You can also back up by hand and restore from iCloud. This storage belongs to your Apple ID; the developer has no access to it.

Only when you choose Export to File, the app puts your data in one file and hands it to the place you pick (Files, Google Drive, AirDrop and so on). You decide where it goes; nothing is sent to the developer.

5. Widgets

What the Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets show, and any chore you mark as done from a widget, is passed between the app and its widgets through an App Group (storage on your iPhone that only this app and its widgets can use). Nothing is sent off your device.

6. In-app purchase

Purchases of SOROSORO Pro are handled by Apple (the App Store). The developer never receives or stores your payment information. Purchases and refunds follow Apple's terms.

7. Rating requests

The app may show the standard iOS rating prompt. This prompt is handled by Apple, and the developer does not receive any information through the app from it.

8. Advertising

The app shows no ads, and therefore collects no information for advertising.

9. Sharing with third parties

Because the app collects no information, there is nothing to share.

10. External links

We use Google Forms for contact. Information you enter in the form is handled under that provider's privacy policy.

11. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no information, it is safe to use at any age.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.

13. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use "Settings > Contact" in the app or the contact form on the support page.
