SOROSORO Support

日本語はこちら

Thank you for using SOROSORO. SOROSORO is an iPhone app that gives each cleaning chore — the bathroom, the kitchen, the toilet — its own meter, so you can see at a glance what needs cleaning soon. Tap a button when you've done it, and you'll know when it's due next.

How it works

1. Choose from 32 common chores, or add your own with a name, a place and how often. You can add your own places, too.
2. Each chore's meter shows how much time is left before it's due, based on when you last did it and how often you want to.
3. When you've cleaned, tap the button to log it. The meter fills back up.
4. On the Log tab, look back at what you did in a monthly calendar.
5. On the Deep clean tab, once-a-year chores get a reminder in the best month. In countries in the Southern Hemisphere, the suggested months are shifted by six months.

Fits your pace

If you actually clean more or less often than the target, SOROSORO suggests adjusting it. One tap sets the target to your real pace.

Notifications

Once a day, at the time you choose (7 AM, 12 PM or 8 PM), one notification lists what's due. All notifications are created on your iPhone, and you can turn them off on the Settings tab.

Moving to a new iPhone

SOROSORO backs up your data to your own iCloud, in a space only this app uses, once a day (you can turn this off in Settings). On the Settings tab you can also Back up now or Restore from iCloud. Bringing your data over to a new iPhone is free.

Not using iCloud? Tap Export to File on the Settings tab to save to Files, Google Drive, AirDrop and more, then tap Import from File on your new iPhone (free). Pro isn't included in the file; use Restore purchase.

Widgets

You can add widgets to your Home Screen (small and medium) and Lock Screen (SOROSORO Pro). On iOS 17 or later, you can mark a chore as done right from the widget.

SOROSORO Pro

SOROSORO is free and has no ads. On the free plan you can add up to 10 chores. All 10 color themes are free.

SOROSORO Pro is a one-time purchase (US$6.99), not a subscription, and supports Family Sharing. It adds:

Unlimited chores (10 on the free plan)
More looks for Home (rings, or grouped by place)
Widgets (Home Screen and Lock Screen)

Purchases are handled by Apple. If you bought it before (after changing phones or reinstalling), go to Settings > Pro and tap Restore purchase under the Buy button. It is tied to your Apple ID, so there is no extra charge.

FAQ

Q. How is the meter worked out?
A. From the date you last did the chore and its target interval (for example, every week), SOROSORO works out how much time is left before it's due. The meter drains day by day and changes from "Clean" to "Soon" to "Due".

Q. I marked a chore as done by mistake
A. Tap "Undo" in the message that appears right after. To delete it later, tap the chore on Home, tap the date under History, and tap "Delete this entry".

Q. I want to log something I did yesterday
A. Tap the chore on Home, then tap "Did it yesterday" or "2 days ago".

Q. How do I change how often a chore is due?
A. Tap the chore on Home and choose under "How often". To change the name or place, tap "Edit name and place".

Q. I'm not getting notifications
A. Please check the following:
· In the iPhone Settings app → SOROSORO → Notifications, notifications are allowed
· On the app's Settings tab, "Remind me when due" is on
Notifications come once a day at the time you choose, and only on days when something is due.

Q. Can I move my data to a new iPhone?
A. SOROSORO backs up to your own iCloud once a day. Use the same Apple ID on your new iPhone and tap "Restore from iCloud" on the Settings tab to bring your data over, free. Not using iCloud? Use Export to File, then Import from File on the new iPhone.

Q. Pro is not showing as purchased
A. Go to Settings > Pro and tap "Restore purchase" under the Buy button. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Q. How do I add a widget?
A. Home Screen: touch and hold an empty area → Edit → Add Widget → SOROSORO. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → widget area → SOROSORO. Widgets are part of SOROSORO Pro.

Q. I can't add any more chores
A. The free plan allows up to 10 chores. With SOROSORO Pro you can add as many as you like.

Q. Are there ads?
A. No.

Q. Where is my data stored?
A. Only on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy.

Q. Is there an Android version?
A. Not at the moment.
